[DEVELOPER NAME] ("we") builds the mobile game **BandPick** (the "App"). This policy
explains what the App does with your information.

## The short version

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information, and there is no account to
create. Every genre and artist is bundled with the App. The only thing loaded from the internet is
**Spotify's own player**, on the results screen, and only when you finish a game.

## What the App stores

Nothing that identifies you. Your game is held in memory only while you are playing; when you close
the App or start a new game, it is gone. There is no account, no profile, and no cloud save.

## What the App sends

Nothing to us. We operate no servers and receive no information about you. The game itself makes no
network requests: every music genre and artist is bundled with the App.

## The Spotify player

The results screen can show a **Spotify player** for the artist you crowned, so you can hear them.
That player is content served by Spotify, not by us, and it is embedded directly in the App:

- Loading it makes your device request it from **Spotify** (`open.spotify.com`), which necessarily
  receives your IP address and information about your device, and may set cookies or similar storage
  for its own player.
- **We receive none of that.** We cannot see what you play, and nothing about your game is sent to
  Spotify — only the artist id of your champion is placed in the player's address.
- Spotify's handling of that data is governed by Spotify's own privacy policy:
  <https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/>.
- You can avoid it entirely: the player only loads if you open a finished game's results screen, and
  the App never plays audio on its own.

Tapping **Open in Spotify** hands the artist to your browser or the Spotify app, exactly like tapping
any other link.

## Permissions

The App requests only `INTERNET`, which is used by the Spotify player and by the links you tap when
sharing a result. Nothing is sent to us.

## Sharing a result

When you tap a share button, the App hands text — and, if you choose, a picture of your result card —
to your device's own share sheet, or opens a website such as WhatsApp, Facebook, Telegram or X in
your browser. **We do not receive any of it.** Whatever you choose to share is governed by the
privacy policy of the app or site you share it to.

The App writes the result card image to the device's cache folder so the share sheet can read it.
You can clear it at any time through your device settings.

## Children

The App is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Third parties

The App contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs. **Spotify** is the
only third party the App ever contacts, and only to display the player described above — we embed
their player as-is and add no tracking of our own.
