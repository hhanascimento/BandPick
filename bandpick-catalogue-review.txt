BANDPICK — CATALOGUE REVIEW

12 genres x 50 acts = 600 slots. Every act appears in exactly one genre.

HOW TO READ THIS
  A plain line is a band.
  [SOLO]        a household-name solo artist kept on purpose (bands-only game).
  [SET BY HAND] the genre was chosen by hand, not by the automatic rule.
  "also in:"    the other genres Wikidata also files this act under. Every
                one of these was decided in favour of the genre it sits in,
                so this is the list to scan when something looks mis-filed.
  The QID is the Wikidata id — the unambiguous way to name an entry.

HOW TO REPLY
  Name the moves you want, e.g. "move 42 in Rock to Indie", or "cut pop
  3, 17". A cut is refilled from the reserves listed under each genre.
